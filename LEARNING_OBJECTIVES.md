# Learning Objectives

After playing OUTPOST, learners should be able to:

1. Explain that an outpost has a limited shared power budget: using more power for one job leaves less for another.
2. Describe why life support matters. Low Life Support makes air and crew health fall.
3. Describe why radiation shielding matters most during solar storms.
4. Recognize delayed results: a greenhouse costs power now but produces food on a later sol.
5. Compare Moon and Mars challenges: long lunar nights use batteries, while Mars dust can reduce solar power and Earth messages can arrive late.
6. Read a simple data label and distinguish a live NASA result from an archived fallback record.
7. Treat failure as evidence: use Mission Replay to identify a turn and trade-off to improve next time.

The game uses plain language, a small glossary, large keyboard-accessible controls, and visual labels that do not rely on color alone.
